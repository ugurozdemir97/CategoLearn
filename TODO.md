# In The Future (Maybe, I am not certain about adding these)

## Quiz and Learning by Repetition
Add a Quiz type. Quizzes will have flashcards with a front and back face. They will include settings to control how frequently they need to be reviewed.  
You can create quizzes and add flashcards inside them to review what you are learning.

## Voice Recording and Images
Add voice recording and sound upload options to card names, field names, and contexts. These can store sound information.  
Field names can also contain images. There should be a “take picture” or “upload” feature. The UI will allow cropping images so they don’t take up too much space, and all pictures will have a standard size.

## Tree Display
Add a screen to show all your folders and files as a tree. It will be difficult to implement, but it would be a nice feature.

## Synchronization
Add synchronization using a Google account or perhaps a custom backend. This way, deleting and reinstalling the app will not cause you to lose your data.  
Basically, it will serve as a backup system.
